2. Essential National Income & Macro Formulas

Expenditure Approach (GDP)

GDP = C + I + G + (X - M)

Gross National Product (GNP)

GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)

Net Domestic Product (NDP)

NDP = GDP - Depreciation (CCA)

GDP Deflator

GDP Deflator = (Nominal GDP / Real GDP) × 100

Consumer Price Index (CPI)

CPI = (Cost of Basket Current / Cost of Basket Base) × 100

Inflation Rate

Rate = [(CPI_t - CPI_t-1) / CPI_t-1] × 100

Unemployment Rate

Rate = (Unemployed / Labor Force) × 100

Labor Force Participation Rate

Rate = (Labor Force / Adult Population) × 100
